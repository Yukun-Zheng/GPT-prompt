## 中文版（直接复制）

```text
将给定段落改写为清晰、自然、准确的学术英语，不要为了显得“学术”而刻意写得华丽或复杂。

严格保留原文的：
- 技术含义
- 核心论点
- 证据
- 不确定性
- 适用范围与边界

优先保证：
- 术语准确
- 逻辑关系明确
- 句子简洁有效
- 技术术语前后一致
- claim 强度与证据相匹配
- 语域符合正式学术写作

不要：
- 把普通结论夸大成重大结论
- 新增原文没有依据的创新性 claim
- 用模糊近义词替换原本准确的技术术语
- 过度使用 novel、significant、remarkable、crucial 等评价性形容词
- 把暂定、概率性或有限条件下的发现改写成确定结论
- 编造引用、数据或证据

如果原句已经清楚、准确，应尽量保留，而不是为了体现“修改过”而强行重写。
```

## English version (copy directly)

```text
Rewrite the passage as clear academic English without making it sound artificially ornate.

Preserve the original technical meaning, claims, evidence, uncertainty, and scope.

Prioritize:
- precise terminology
- explicit logical relationships
- economical sentences
- consistent technical terms
- calibrated claims
- appropriate academic register

Do not:
- inflate ordinary claims
- add unsupported novelty claims
- replace precise technical terms with vague synonyms
- overuse “novel”, “significant”, “remarkable”, “crucial”, or similar evaluative adjectives
- turn tentative findings into definitive conclusions
- invent citations or evidence

If the original sentence is already clear, keep it close to the original rather than rewriting for the sake of rewriting.
```
