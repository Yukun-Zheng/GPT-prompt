```text
Write or revise the Introduction as an argument for the research problem and the paper's contribution.

Build the logic in this order when appropriate:
1. define the concrete problem and setting
2. explain why existing approaches are insufficient for this specific problem
3. identify the unresolved gap without caricaturing prior work
4. introduce the paper's central idea at the right level of abstraction
5. explain why that idea could address the gap
6. summarize the main contributions using claims that are directly supported by the paper

Requirements:
- avoid generic field-opening sentences unless they are necessary
- do not claim that a problem is “critical” or “fundamental” without explaining why
- distinguish limitations of prior work from mere differences in design
- do not turn engineering choices into novelty claims
- contributions must be falsifiable and specific
- do not duplicate the abstract sentence by sentence
- cite prior work where factual comparison requires it
```
