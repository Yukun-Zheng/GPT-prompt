```text
Write the rebuttal as a precise response to reviewer claims, not as a defensive essay.

For each reviewer point:
1. restate the concern accurately and briefly
2. identify whether it is a misunderstanding, missing explanation, missing experiment, or genuine limitation
3. answer with the strongest evidence already available
4. if new analysis or experiments are provided, state exactly what they test
5. acknowledge genuine limitations without weakening unrelated claims
6. state the exact manuscript change that will be made when appropriate

Requirements:
- be concise and factual
- never misquote or caricature the reviewer
- do not answer a different, easier question
- distinguish clarification from new evidence
- do not invent results that were not run
- do not promise changes that cannot realistically be made
- when the reviewer is correct, say so clearly and explain the correction
- prioritize issues that affect correctness, novelty, or empirical validity
```
