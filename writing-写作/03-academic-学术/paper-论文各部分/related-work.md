## 中文版（直接复制）

```text
撰写或修改 Related Work，使它成为不同研究路线之间的结构化比较，而不是文献清单。

对于每一条相关研究路线，说明：
- 它试图解决什么问题
- 它的核心机制是什么，抽象层级应与当前论文比较需要相匹配
- 它已经建立了什么证据或能力
- 与当前论文真正相关的限制、边界或设计差异是什么

只要条件允许，优先按概念关系、机制关系或研究问题来组织论文，而不是按照发表年份排列。

要求：
- 不要歪曲或弱化前作来衬托当前论文
- 区分“并非为我们的 setting 设计”和“在我们的 setting 中已经被证明失败”
- 区分“缺少相关证据”和“存在失败证据”
- 当多篇工作共享同一核心思想时，不要机械地逐篇一句话罗列
- 保留准确引用，不得编造参考文献
- 不要只因为方法名称相似就将其归为同类
- 结尾应准确说明当前论文在已有研究版图中的位置，而不是泛泛声称“优于现有方法”
```

## English version (copy directly)

```text
Write or revise Related Work as a structured comparison of research lines, not a bibliography dump.

For each relevant research line:
- state the problem it addresses
- summarize the mechanism at the right abstraction level
- explain what it establishes
- identify the limitation or difference that is directly relevant to this paper

Organize papers by conceptual relationship rather than by publication year whenever possible.

Requirements:
- do not misrepresent prior work to make the current paper look stronger
- distinguish “not designed for our setting” from “fails in our setting”
- distinguish missing evidence from demonstrated failure
- avoid one-sentence paper-by-paper enumeration when several works share one idea
- preserve accurate citations and do not invent references
- end by locating the current paper precisely in this landscape, not by claiming generic superiority
```
